# PetClinic User Manual

PetClinic is a small veterinary practice management application. You can use it to keep records of pet owners, the pets in their care, the veterinarians on staff, the specialties those vets practice, and the visits the pets make to the clinic.

## Getting around

The top navigation bar is always visible. *Home* takes you back to the welcome screen. *Owners*, *Visits*, *Pet Types*, and *Specialties* are direct links to each area's list. *Veterinarians* is a dropdown with two entries: *All* (the vet list) and *Add New* (the add-vet form).

## Owners

This is where you manage the clinic's clients. Each owner record holds contact details and the list of pets they've brought in. From an owner's record you can also register a new pet for them — see [Pets](#pets) — and every pet's visit history is shown right there too — see [Visits](#visits).

### Viewing the list

![](screenshots/owners-list.png)

Type part of a last name into the search box and click *Find Owner* to filter the list; leave the box empty and click *Find Owner* to see everyone. Each row shows the owner's name, address, phone number, and the names of their pets. Click an owner's name to open their record.

### Creating a new owner

![](screenshots/owners-create.png)

Click *Add Owner* from the owner list to register a new client. Fill in name, address, city, and telephone, then save to create the record and land on the owner's list.

### Looking at an owner's record

![](screenshots/owners-detail.png)

The owner's record shows their contact details followed by a *Pets and Visits* section listing every pet they own. For each pet you can see its name, birth date, and type, and the table of past visits underneath it. From here you can edit or delete the owner, add a new pet, or act on any individual pet or visit (edit, delete, or add a new visit).

### Editing an owner

![](screenshots/owners-edit.png)

Click *Edit Owner* on the owner's record to open this form, pre-filled with their current details. Update any field and save to apply the change, or go back to discard it.

## Pets

Pets belong to owners, so you always reach a pet's forms starting from that owner's record — see [Owners](#owners).

### Adding a pet

![](screenshots/pets-add.png)

From an owner's record, click *Add New Pet*. The owner is already selected; fill in the pet's name, birth date, and type, then click *Save Pet*. The new pet appears in the owner's *Pets and Visits* list.

### Editing a pet

![](screenshots/pets-edit.png)

Click *Edit Pet* next to a pet on its owner's record to open this form, pre-filled with the pet's current name, birth date, and type. Save to apply the change, or go back to discard it.

## Visits

Visits record each time a pet is seen at the clinic. You add a visit from the pet's entry on its owner's record — see [Owners](#owners) — but you can browse every visit across every pet from the clinic-wide Visits screen.

### Viewing the list

![](screenshots/visits-list.png)

The Visits screen lists every recorded visit clinic-wide, newest first, with its date, description, the pet seen, and a link to that pet's owner. Click the owner's name to jump to their record.

### Adding a visit

![](screenshots/visits-add.png)

Click *Add Visit* next to a pet on its owner's record. The pet is shown for reference; enter the visit date and a description, then click *Add Visit* to save. The pet's list of previous visits appears below the form.

### Editing a visit

![](screenshots/visits-edit.png)

Click *Edit Visit* next to a visit in a pet's visit history (on the owner's record) to open this form, pre-filled with the visit's date and description. Save to apply the change, or go back to discard it.

## Pet Types

Pet Types is the catalog of species/breeds (cat, dog, hamster, and so on) offered when registering a pet.

### Viewing the list

![](screenshots/pettypes-list.png)

Every pet type is listed by name. Use *Edit* or *Delete* next to a row to change or remove it, or *Add* to create a new one.

### Creating a new pet type

![](screenshots/pettypes-create.png)

Click *Add* from the pet types list, enter the new type's name, and save. It becomes available the next time you register a pet.

### Editing a pet type

![](screenshots/pettypes-edit.png)

Click *Edit* next to a pet type to open this form, pre-filled with its current name. Save to apply the change, or go back to discard it.

## Veterinarians

This area manages the clinic's staff of vets and the specialties each one practices.

### Viewing the list

![](screenshots/vets-list.png)

Each row shows a vet's name and their specialties. Use *Edit Vet* or *Delete Vet* next to a row, or *Add Vet* to register a new one.

### Creating a new vet

![](screenshots/vets-create.png)

Click *Add Vet* from the vet list (or *Add New* under the Veterinarians menu). Enter the vet's first and last name, choose their specialties, and save.

### Editing a vet

![](screenshots/vets-edit.png)

Click *Edit Vet* next to a vet to open this form, pre-filled with their name and specialties. Adjust the fields and save, or go back to discard the change.
