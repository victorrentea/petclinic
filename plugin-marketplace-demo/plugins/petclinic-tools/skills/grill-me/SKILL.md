Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. 
Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. 
For each question, provide your recommended answer.
Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering😵‍💫.
If a question can be answered by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc), look it up rather than asking me. 
The decisions, though, are mine — ask one to me and wait for my answer.
